### Textbooks

**1. Digital Design**<br/>
BY: Morris Mano and Michael Ciletti - Chapter 5 (Combinational Logic) and Chapter 6 (Sequential Logic)<br/>
_Comprehensive coverage of ALU design, arithmetic logic units, and processor components._

**2. Digital Logic and Computer Design**<br/>
BY: Morris Mano - Chapter 5<br/>
_Fundamental concepts of arithmetic logic units and their implementation in digital systems._

**3. Computer Organization and Design**<br/>
BY: David A. Patterson and John L. Hennessy - Chapter 3 (Arithmetic for Computers)<br/>
_Hardware perspective on ALU operations, arithmetic implementations, and processor design._

**4. Digital Systems: Principles and Applications**<br/>
BY: Ronald J. Tocci, Neal S. Widmer, and Gregory L. Moss - Chapter 7<br/>
_Excellent coverage of arithmetic logic units and their role in digital processing systems._

**5. Digital Electronics: Principles, Devices and Applications**<br/>
BY: Anil K. Maini - Chapter 6<br/>
_Practical approach to ALU design with detailed analysis of arithmetic and logical operations._

**6. Computer System Architecture**<br/>
BY: Morris Mano - Chapter 8<br/>
_Detailed coverage of ALU design principles and their integration in computer systems._
